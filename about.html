Hi, I'm the mama behind the craft table

Mama Craft Haus started at the kitchen table with a glass, a steady hand and a friend's wedding toast. What began as a gift turned into a little business, and now every order is still made by hand at home.

I love helping people mark the big moments: weddings, a baby's first Christmas, Easter mornings, family trips. Each piece is personalized just for you, and I'll always send a proof before I start.
